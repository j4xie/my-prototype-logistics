## Scope discipline (Apr 23 2026 audit)

Spec §5 lists 9 modules for v1.2 flip. Post-audit, only 2 (趋势, 销售订单) have the Silver data to do a meaningful flip today. Rushing the other 7 would mean either (a) adding empty placeholders or (b) rushing Silver schema design. Both are worse than honest deferral.

v1.1 + v1.2 combined surface after this sprint: **5 flipped modules** (财务报表, 分析概览, KPI看板, 趋势, 销售订单). That is what v1.1+v1.2 ship to production today.
